Giving FAQs

Is my giving tax deductible?

Tax treatment of donations varies, so please contact our church treasurer for a giving statement and guidance for your situation.

Can I give anonymously?

Yes. Simply mark your envelope or EFT reference as "Anonymous" and leave out any personal details.

Can I set up recurring giving?

Yes. Speak to our church treasurer about setting up a recurring EFT or debit order for regular giving.

Where can I get a giving statement?

Contact the church office and we'll issue a statement of your giving for the year.
