Meet Our Pastors

Pastor David Mensah

Senior Pastor

Pastor David Mensah serves as our Senior Pastor, bringing over 15 years of pastoral experience in gospel ministry, family counseling, and community outreach. Passionate about teaching the Word of God and sharing the Blessed Hope, Pastor David dedicates his ministry to empowering members to live out Christ's love daily. He holds a Master of Divinity from Adventist University and enjoys spending quality time with his family, reading, and organizing community health expos.

Amma Osei

Outreach & Evangelism Pastor

Amma Osei leads our Community Outreach and Evangelism Ministries. She is dedicated to connecting the church with the broader community through compassionate service, Bible study initiatives, and food pantry programs. Pastor Amma believes that meeting people's physical and emotional needs is at the heart of Christ's mission. In her free time, she enjoys gardening, writing, and hosting fellowship meals.

Pastor Emmanuel Kwesi

Associate Pastor, Youth & Family

Pastor Emmanuel Kwesi serves as our Associate Pastor, focusing on Youth Ministry, Family Life, and Young Adult Discipleship. With a heart for mentoring the next generation, Pastor Emmanuel is committed to creating engaging, Christ-centered spaces where young people can grow in faith and service. He holds a B.A. in Theology and loves music ministry, outdoor camping adventures, and sports evangelism.

Our Ministries

Children's

Our Children's Ministry provides a safe, nurturing environment for the youngest members of our church family. From newborn daycare through Grade 5, children enjoy age-appropriate Bible lessons, songs, and activities that build a foundation of faith. Grade 5–11 continues this journey with deeper Bible study, mentorship, and fellowship designed to guide pre-teens and teenagers as they grow in their walk with God.

Youth

Our Youth Ministry creates a space for teens and young adults to wrestle with real questions, build real friendships, and grow a faith that's truly their own — through Bible study, worship nights, camps and outreach.

Women's
