Meet Our Pastors

Pastor David Nkosi

Pastor David Nkosi serves as our Senior Pastor, bringing over 15 years of pastoral experience in gospel ministry, family counseling, and community outreach. Passionate about teaching the Word of God and sharing the Blessed Hope, Pastor David dedicates his ministry to empowering members to live out Christ's love daily. He holds a Master of Divinity from Adventist University and enjoys spending quality time with his family, reading, and organizing community health expos.

Pastor Sarah Jenkins

Pastor Sarah Jenkins leads our Community Outreach and Evangelism Ministries. She is dedicated to connecting the church with the broader community through compassionate service, Bible study initiatives, and food pantry programs. Pastor Sarah believes that meeting people's physical and emotional needs is at the heart of Christ's mission. In her free time, she enjoys gardening, writing, and hosting fellowship meals.

Pastor Michael Evans

Pastor Michael Evans serves as our Associate Pastor, focusing on Youth Ministry, Family Life, and Young Adult Discipleship. With a heart for mentoring the next generation, Pastor Michael is committed to creating engaging, Christ-centered spaces where young people can grow in faith and service. He holds a B.A. in Theology and loves music ministry, outdoor camping adventures, and sports evangelism.

Our Ministries

Children's

Our Children's Ministry provides a safe, nurturing environment for the youngest members of our church family. From newborn daycare through Grade 5, children enjoy age-appropriate Bible lessons, songs, and activities that build a foundation of faith. Grade 5–11 continues this journey with deeper Bible study, mentorship, and fellowship designed to guide pre-teens and teenagers as they grow in their walk with God.
